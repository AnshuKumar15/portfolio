MindMate++ is an AI-powered mental wellness and student companion platform designed to help young adults tackle academic burnout, anxiety, and emotional distress. MindMate++ bridges this gap by providing an empathetic voice and chat companion across English, Hindi, and Kannada, flagging real-time distress and self-harm keywords for rapid intervention, and preventing exam panic through automated, burnout-free study scheduling.

HYBRID RAG FOR PDF Q&A

A high-precision document intelligence system combining keyword and semantic vector search with neural reranking to deliver grounded, hallucination-free answers from complex PDFs.
OVERVIEW

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Standard vector search often fails on dense technical documents because it misses exact keyword matches, loses context across long pages, and risks AI hallucination. This project addresses these challenges by uniting lexical search (BM25) with semantic embeddings (FAISS) and cross-encoder reranking, ensuring pinpoint answer accuracy with verifiable, page-level source citations.

KEY WORK

WHAT I BUILT

  • Hybrid Search & Reranking: Combined BM25 keyword matching with FAISS vector search and FlashRank reranking for maximum retrieval precision.
  • Hierarchical Document Chunking: Multi-level PDF parsing that preserves section context, tables, and document hierarchy.
  • Grounded Answers & Citations: Integrated Gemini Flash with page-level citations and SHA-256 caching for instant, verifiable responses.
